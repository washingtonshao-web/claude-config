# Desktop apps (Falcon Toolkit)

A desktop window is read at arm's length for hours, so legibility comes before everything.

- **Text size:** body and table text at least 14px at 100% scaling, labels at least 13px, nothing under 12px. If a view feels cramped, remove something; do not shrink the type.
- **One type scale:** at most four sizes in a window, one weight for body and one for emphasis.
- **Colour:** the kit palette. Status (running, waiting, failed) always has a word or an icon beside its colour.
- **No banners and no marketing copy.** The window opens on what I do most; nothing sits above it that does nothing. One line of help at most, never repeated.
- **Density:** rows tall enough to click (32px or more), consistent padding, numbers right-aligned with tabular figures.
- **State is visible without reading:** what is running, what needs me, what failed, each in a fixed place.
- **Check it:** screenshot the real window at 100% and at 150% scaling and look at both before saying it is done.
