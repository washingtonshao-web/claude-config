# The shelf: pages I approved

These are the pages I have said I like. Look at the screenshots before designing a showcase page, and give the reviewer two of them to compare against (`review.md`). A page joins this list only when I say I like it; do not add pages on your own.

Screenshots are in `shelf/` beside this file: `<name>-desktop.jpg` (1440×900) and `<name>-phone.jpg` (440×760).

Report look: nothing is on the shelf yet. The Economist-style chart samples I picked on 2026-10-02 (linked in `charts.md`) are the reference until I approve a report page.

What these have in common, and what a new showcase page should share with them: the first screen is one image or one map, not a layout; the title belongs to the picture; the colours come from the subject; there is very little text before the first scroll.
